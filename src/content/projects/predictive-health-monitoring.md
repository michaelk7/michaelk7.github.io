## Context

A Bradley University project built with data from OSF HealthCare. The goal was to flag patients at risk of **diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)** early.

## What I built

- A machine-learning model trained to predict DKA risk from patient data.
- A Django web application to make predictions available to users.
- Deployment on AWS.
